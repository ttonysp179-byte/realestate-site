LOAN PROGRAMS

Financing Options Explained

Understanding your loan options is the first step. Here are the most common programs available to buyers.

FHA Loans

Low down payment options (as low as 3.5%) perfect for first-time buyers. More flexible credit requirements make homeownership accessible.

Conventional Loans

Competitive rates with various down payment options. Ideal for buyers with strong credit who want the best long-term terms.

First-Time Buyer Programs

Special grants, down payment assistance, and reduced closing costs available through state and local programs in California.

PRE-APPROVAL

Understanding DTI & Pre-Approval

What is DTI?

Your Debt-to-Income ratio (DTI) compares your monthly debt payments to your gross monthly income. Most lenders prefer a DTI below 43%, though some programs allow higher.

Why Get Pre-Approved?

Pre-approval gives you a clear picture of what you can afford, shows sellers you're a serious buyer, and speeds up the closing process.
