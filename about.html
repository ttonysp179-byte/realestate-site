MY STORY

Why I Do What I Do

Growing up in the Whittier area, I've always loved the sense of community here — the tree-lined streets, the local shops on Greenleaf, the feeling that your neighbors truly know you.

When I helped my first clients — a young couple buying their first home in La Mirada — I realized that real estate isn't just about houses. It's about helping people build a life, create memories, and invest in their future.

That experience shaped my mission: to make the home buying and selling process clear, honest, and even enjoyable. I specialize in working with first-time buyers, growing families, and anyone who wants a realtor who truly cares about their outcome.

Backed by the trusted Better Homes & Gardens Real Estate brand, I bring national resources with a deeply local touch. Whether you're looking in Whittier, La Habra, Hacienda Heights, or anywhere in the San Gabriel Valley, I'm here to help.
